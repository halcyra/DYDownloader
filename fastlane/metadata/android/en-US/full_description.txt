An open-source, free and local Android app for downloading watermark-free Douyin/TikTok original images, albums, live photos, videos, account posts, and collection posts.

Parse shared links, download in batches, preview locally, and customize file names. Direct platform requests, with no third-party parsing service.

Some content requires sign-in. Cookies stay on the device and are only sent to the corresponding platform. Both platforms are proprietary network services.

For learning and lawful personal use. Respect copyright and platform rules.
